## 1. Who offers хоумстейджинг / предпродажная подготовка in Krasnodar (direct and indirect competitors)

### Takeaway
Krasnodar has a small, fragmented field of **direct** stagers: about 6–8 identifiable studios or solo stagers (Homely Style, Remdizayn, Идиллия, Инвест Дизайн, AmorStaging, «Выгодная упаковка квартиры», Home Staging Pro (Krasnodar), ЗДЕСЬ/YourApart). Most publish **per-object fees of ~9–80 тыс. ₽** for the staging service itself, not counting furniture. A furnishing-for-rent ("комплектация под ключ") tier runs at ~120–150 тыс. ₽ agency fee plus 550–700 тыс. ₽ of goods. **Indirect** competition is large: Profi.ru alone lists 1 388 interior designers in Krasnodar under "хоум-стейджинг". There are also renovation firms that sell staging, short-term-rental operators, flippers and newly trained course graduates. No large agency (Этажи, Самолёт Плюс, Инком) was found publishing a **Krasnodar** staging price.

### Inferences
- The **published Krasnodar fee band for "staging only" (no furniture purchase)** clusters at **~25–80 тыс. ₽ per flat**: Remdizayn 25–80k, Homely Style 35–70k (студия 35k, 1-к/евро-2 55k), Идиллия от 30k turnkey. Per-m² pricing runs from **350 ₽/м² (one-day photo-staging) to 750 ₽/м² (full)** at Инвест Дизайн, with minimums of 15–24k. The 93.ru quote of ~5 000 ₽/м² for новостройки almost certainly **includes furniture/décor purchase** (that is the only way it fits next to the other numbers); treat it as an all-in budget, not a fee.
- An **entry tier exists at 3–5 тыс. ₽ (consultation)** and **9–15 тыс. ₽ (express, 1–3 days)**. A family business with its own crews would compete against these as a lead-in product.
- Remdizayn shows that at least one Krasnodar repair/design firm already markets staging. The "repair + staging" combination is therefore **not unique** in Krasnodar (see Q5).
- The rent-furnishing segment (Home Staging Pro, ЗДЕСЬ, AmorStaging) is a distinct, higher-ticket market: 120–150k fee on top of 550–700k of goods.

## 2. Russian benchmarks (Moscow, SPb, Sochi, Rostov, Yekaterinburg; large agencies; media)

### Takeaway
Moscow full-service staging is published at **~70–200 тыс. ₽ per object**, with consultations at **15 тыс. ₽** at a top agency. SPb studios price **per m² (≈700–5 000 ₽/м²)** with **minimums of ~50–60 тыс. ₽**. Sochi is close to Krasnodar at the entry level, but furnishing packages start around **400 тыс. ₽**. Rostov and Yekaterinburg aggregator averages are **~11–12 тыс. ₽** per service. Media rules of thumb: the stager's fee is **0,3–1% of the property value**, and staging spend excluding the fee is **≤1–2%** of value.

### Inferences
- Krasnodar's published fee band (25–80k) sits **below Moscow (70–200k) and SPb (min 50–60k)** and is roughly in line with the "regions 10–15% cheaper than Moscow" claim only at the low end. More plausibly, Krasnodar is **~40–60% below Moscow** at like-for-like scope.
- The **0,3–1% of property value** fee rule, applied to Krasnodar's new-build average (≈9,5 млн ₽ per Cian; see Q3 context), gives **~28–95 тыс. ₽**, consistent with local studio prices.
- Per-m² pricing is common in SPb and Sochi (700–5 500 ₽/м²) but rare in Krasnodar (only Инвест Дизайн, 350–750 ₽/м²). This could be an opening for transparent per-m² or per-room packages.

## 5. Gaps in the local market (evidence vs hypothesis)

### Takeaway
The evidence does **not** support the claim that "nobody in Krasnodar combines repairs + staging + photos under one contract":
- Remdizayn markets staging from a repair/design base.
- Homely Style bundles a final photoshoot.
- «Выгодная упаковка» lists «глубокая уборка и мелкий ремонт».

What *is* evidenced is narrower: no staging-furniture rental supplier, few transparent fixed packages, and no big agency with a published Krasnodar staging offer.

### Cited Findings
